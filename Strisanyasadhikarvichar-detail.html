Publications



Strisanyasadhikarvichar

The only proof of the existence of religion is only the Vedas. No other principle can be accepted as they are baseless without support of the Vedas. Presently voice is raised for the equal rights of women. It gives an impression that women are deprived from religion and rituals. It presents the wrong perspective of the Vedas . The Vedas have given equal rights to women since ancient times . In the sixth chapter of Mimansathe equal rights of men and women are n described to be as strong and steady as the Himalayas.

Shri KamlakantTripathiwrote this scripture in Sanskrit ,which is being interpreted in lucid language by Shri JagadguruRamanujacharya Shri Raghavacharyaji . This book is first of its kind in the legacy of essay books ,which brings out the real nature of religion and rights that women are entitled to since a long time . It also helps the scholars to see the true nature of religion for women ,giving up the misconceptions regarding it.
