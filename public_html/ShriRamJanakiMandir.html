    Shri Ram Janaki Mandir

  • Shri Ram Janaki Mandir is situated at the distance of 30Km. from Ayodhya. It is known to be one of the ancient temples there. Since 2018 Swamiji is the Servarohav Mahant, taking care of the restoration of the temple. He is guiding the project. The Temple is situated in the 3 Acre area of the land.
  • Future Plan are to restore the old temple and renovate it. Expansion of shri ved vidyalaya and well equipped guest house will be done in this premises.
