    Restoration Of Shri Ramlala Devsthanam

  • Background:
    Shri Ramlala Devsthanam is an ancient temple, in which idols of deities are in their childhood form (Balvigrah Swarup). In the year 1917 Swamiji Shri Raghavacharyji established Shri Ramlala Trust .He is the head of the trust. Shri Swamiji became Sarvarahakar of Shri Ramlalasadan Devasthan in 2012. The prime purpose of establishing this trust is the restoration of the old temple.
  • Prime Concept:
    During his pilgrimage to the South India in2009, Swamiji made a pledge to start Narad pancharatr aagam rituals, Utsav, pooja in Shriramlala temple too. He dreamt to make a temple after the South Indian structure, with Gopur, Shikhar and Mandap His dream is coming into reality and a reincarnation of the old temple is taking shape in 15000 sq. ft. area.

  • Future Plans:
    Once the new temple will be completed, all the Utsavas will be celebrated as are performed in the South Indian temples. It will include Rathyatra, Palakhi, Bramhotsav and many more. Vasantmandapam will be the place where Utsavmurti idols will be worshipped on Utsava celebrations. Yajyas will be performed in the Yajya shala. A separate Mandapam is planned for the musicians, who will perform during temple festivals.
  • Shri Yamunacharya Athiti Gruha (Guest House):
    The guest house will be well connected to the temple. It is planned as two -storey building. There will be five rooms for the students, kitchen and dining room at the ground floor. One room will be for VIP guests. On the first floor there will be two dormitories – for male and female devotees. Separate guests rooms are planned for devotees. Along with one family room, there will be a VIP room. The guest house will be equipped with all amenities and lift facility.
    The donors to the guest house can have their names or the names of their family members engraved at the prominent place in the premises. They can also get benefit of the other facilities available to them.
