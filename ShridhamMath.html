    Ved Vidyalay & Annakshetra by Shridham Math, Ayodhya
  • Background:
    In the year 1980 the land of Shridhammath was purchased by Shri Jairamdasji Maharaj .The Math was build by the help and contribution of the devotees in Ayodhya. The idols of Shriram, Sita and Laxman are the main deities in the temple. Shrimaharajji dedicated all the property of the temple to the dirties and preferred to work as Sarvaharakar, that is the caretaker according to the religious tradition of Ayodhya Sarvarahkar is works only as the head and the caretaker and he does not have the power to sell the property , that comes under the temple . He has the authority to appoint new Sarvarahkar when the time comes. Shri Jairamdasji Swamiji considered Swamiji Raghavacharyaji as the suitable person to bear the responsibility of Sarvarahkar and appointed him to that position in the year 1999.
  • Purpose:
    The main purpose of the Shridhammath is the service and worship of the Shrimurties. Along with it, the food centre / Annakshetra of the temple arranges Satvik food for the devotees, who visit Ayodhya .It also gives lodging facility to them. In the year 2003 cow shelter /Goshala -Shri Nandani Goshala -started auto work with the purpose to save cows. In 2011 Shri Ramanuj Vedvidyalaya came into existence with Sanskrit and Vedic educational purpose. Both these projects work under Shridhammath.
  • Future Plans:
    Shridhammath is situated near to Shriramjanmbhumi and Shrihanumanghadhi. Ayodhya is turning into a new centre of Hindu faith .It is expected that a larger number of devotees will visit Ayodhya in near future. The future plan is to make arrangement of food for 100-150 devotees. Presently the food expenditure is Rs. 50000/ monthly.

    Shrinandani Goshala has 32 cows, which will increase to 50. Yearly expenditure on a single cow is Rs. 21000/.

    Shri Ramanuj Vedvidhyalaya has 17 students. Monthly expenditure on a student is Rs. 2500/ as all the facilities are free to the students.
  • The devotees can donate in all mentioned projects. The donation amount is exempted from income tax (CIT Exemption, Lucknow /80G /2018-19/10207)
  • The devotees can donate from abroad too. The donor will be given proper receipt, all the details through photos and videos.
  • Future Plan are to restore this old temple and renovate it in a ultra modern building for the piligrims who are going to visit Ayodhya. Expansion of ved vidyalaya can be done in this new building.
